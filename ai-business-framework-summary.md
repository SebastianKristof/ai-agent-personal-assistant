# Greg Isenberg's AI Business Framework

## AI Business Opportunity
- We're entering an era of "smart software" with human-like intelligence vs. previous "dumb software" that required manual inputs
- Anyone can create AI businesses in 2023-2025 without technical background
- Companies making $50K+/month are being built with simple AI frameworks
- The combination of global reach (social media) + AI automation creates unprecedented opportunity

## 5-Step Framework for AI Startups

### 1. Identify Painful Workflows
- Find specific tedious workflows that people would pay to fix
- Look for "export buttons" in software - each one represents potential $1-100M business
- Best ideas come from people's current job frustrations
- Example: Icon AI - automates Facebook/Instagram ad creation process
- Audit your own work-life for tedious tasks you dislike doing

### 2. Launch MVP Fast
- Create a "Minimum Lovable Product" quickly rather than perfecting it
- Use tools like Replit, Bolt, V0, and Manis to prototype rapidly
- Get something working in 24 hours that would have cost $100k+ previously
- Iterate based on user feedback rather than trying to build perfect product first
- Embrace being a beginner and learning new tools

### 3. Grow Distribution Daily
- Post content consistently to build audience before/while building product
- New startups should follow "ACP Framework": Audience → Community → Product
- Pick one content format for each business day and test what works
- Set meaningful goals beyond just follower count (email list size, revenue)
- Look for content "spikes" and double down on successful formats

### 4. Nail Retention & Core Loop
- Focus on keeping users engaged, not just acquiring them
- Combine both behavioral analytics (numbers) and attitudinal analytics (customer feedback)
- Successful companies like Calendly focus deeply on one core feature
- "Data informs, not drives" - don't let metrics alone dictate product decisions
- When you have product-market fit, people will "scream it at you"

### 5. Partner with Creators to Scale
- Leverage creator partnerships for faster market entry
- Offer revenue share deals (25-50%) to creators with relevant audiences
- Cold DM outreach works - use personalized video messages to stand out
- Focus on providing value to potential partners
- Building relationships is crucial for scaling business

## AI Tools for Building
- V0 (vzero.dev) - AI product designer that can create websites from text prompts
- Manis - Chinese AI agent platform that can research, design products, and create marketing plans
- These tools dramatically reduce the cost and time to launch new products

## Success Mindset
- Embrace curiosity and willingness to experiment with new tools
- Don't be afraid to "get your hands dirty" with new technology
- Start broad when building an audience, then narrow down
- Always focus on providing value to users and partners
